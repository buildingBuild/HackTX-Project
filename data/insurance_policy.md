# Insurance Levels

## Silver

Essential protection for common accidents and basic roadside needs.

- **Deductible:** $1,000

### Coverage

- Liability
- Collision
- Roadside assistance for a jump-start, spare-tire installation, or towing

### Not covered

- Replacement batteries and tires
- Cosmetic damage, including minor scratches
- Mechanical failures caused by wear or breakdown

## Gold

Expanded protection for accidents, theft, weather damage, and temporary
transportation.

- **Deductible:** $500

### Coverage

- All Silver benefits
- Comprehensive coverage
- Glass repair
- Rental-car reimbursement

### Not covered

- Replacement batteries and tires
- Cosmetic damage, including minor scratches
- Mechanical failures caused by wear or breakdown

## Platinum

The broadest protection, including eligible repair and replacement benefits.

- **Deductible:** $250

### Coverage

- All Gold benefits
- OEM replacement parts
- Gap coverage
- New-car replacement
- Eligible replacement batteries
- Road-hazard tire replacement
- Minor cosmetic scratch repair
- Mechanical-breakdown coverage for eligible internal components

### Conditions and exclusions

- Benefits require diagnosis and approval.
- The deductible applies to eligible replacement or repair claims.
- Routine maintenance, normal wear, and damage caused by neglect are not covered.
